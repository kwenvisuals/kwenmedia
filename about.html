The journey

How I got here.

01

The borrowed MacBook

It started with a loan from my mom. I used it to buy a 13-inch M1 MacBook from 2020 with 16GB of RAM, a machine I couldn't afford yet, for a skill I didn't have yet.

02

Self-taught, one week in

No school, no mentor. Just free lessons on TikTok and YouTube. I started with CapCut, and after about a week of practice every day, I was confident enough to go looking for work.

03

Knocking on every door

I joined every Facebook group for editors and content teams and messaged anyone who was hiring. About 1 in 10 replied, because nobody knew who I was. So I took every job I could get, mostly talking-head videos, and priced well below the market so people would take a chance on someone with nothing to show yet.

04

Leveling up to DaVinci

A few months in, a colleague showed me DaVinci Resolve. I was hooked. In about a month I'd moved my whole workflow over and learned color grading and motion graphics. That's where my edits started to look different.

05

The 1-star review

On Upwork I built my profile from zero: YouTube videos, talking heads, anything I could do well. I chased 5 stars on every job. Then one project went wrong, there was a misunderstanding, and the client left me 1 star. It hurt my profile, and it hurt more personally. I felt like I wasn't good enough. But I didn't let it go. I kept reaching out until he gave me a second project. I delivered it fast and for free. He removed the review. That one taught me more than any tutorial: own the mistake, then outwork it.

06

Finding real estate

After more than a year, real estate content on social media exploded with a fresh, high-energy editing style. I wanted in. I learned Final Cut Pro, and within 3 days of learning and testing it was working. From then on I focused on real estate only.

07

Kwen Media

That's how Kwen Media started. Today I edit for agents and media teams in the US, Canada and Australia, look after every client I already have, and keep looking for new ones. The goal now is bigger than me: to grow Kwen Media into a team that edits with the same care I learned the hard way.

What I believe

Quality. Always first.

Every edit is my reputation

I learned that from a single 1-star review. Every video gets the care of the one that earned my second chance.

Own it, then fix it

If something isn't right, I say so and make it right, fast. Clear communication beats excuses.

Never stop learning

CapCut, then DaVinci, then Final Cut. Styles change fast on social media, so I keep learning to stay ahead of them.
